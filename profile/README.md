  We study how each party keeps control, and we ship the tools.

<details>
<summary><b>Research directions</b></summary>
<br>

| Direction | In one line | Topics |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Data & models** | *Data stays put; capability flows.* | federated and continual learning for foundation models · controllable secure aggregation · poisoning and backdoor defense · machine unlearning |
| **Agents** | *Agents may act on their own; their permissions stay bounded.* | privacy for prompts sent to frontier LLMs · agent memory and delegation security · multi-agent risk evaluation |
| **Foundations** | *AI to engineer cryptography; cryptography to protect AI.* | functional and homomorphic encryption · TEE-assisted confidential aggregation · cryptographic tooling for ML |

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How we work</b></summary>
<br>

**Local first** · **measured, not asserted** · **reproducible** · **open**. Sensitive data stays on its owner's side; every privacy claim ships with an evaluation; configs, seeds, and artifacts make results replayable; prototypes stay open to inspect and extend.

</details>
